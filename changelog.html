Release notes

Every version of Aurora AI Player, newest first.

1.1.0Mac App StoreNovember 2026

First release on the Mac App Store.

  • Watching is free for everyone: every format, scrub previews, playlists, subtitle files.
  • Aurora Pro, a one-time purchase with a 14-day free trial, unlocks the language-learning tools: AI subtitles, live translation and dual subtitles, dictionary, repeat line, sentence notebook with Anki export, subtitle styles, .srt export.
  • AI subtitles now cover the whole video on long MP4 and MKV files.
  • Optional Claude translation with your own Anthropic API key, with a model picker and usage estimate. Press T to retranslate a line.
  • Open videos straight from Finder: double-click, Open With, or drop them on the icon. Opening a file while Aurora is running reuses the window.
  • The app is sandboxed and asks before reading a folder.
  • Settings reorganised into General, Subtitles, Aurora Pro and About.
  • Built for Apple silicon with an LGPL-clean libmpv and FFmpeg 8. See Open source.
1.0.3October 2026

Pre-release builds distributed directly before the App Store launch. Superseded by 1.1.0.
