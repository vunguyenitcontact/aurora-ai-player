Privacy Policy

Aurora AI Player for macOS · Effective 6 November 2026

The short version

Aurora AI Player does not collect, store or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers operated by us. Everything the app does with your videos happens on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your videos and subtitle files are read only from the files and folders you open yourself. macOS keeps that access restricted to those items (App Sandbox).
  • AI subtitles are generated on-device by Apple Speech or Whisper. Audio never leaves your Mac.
  • Apple Translation, the dictionary, your sentence notebook, watch history and settings are stored locally in the app's own container.

When the app uses the network

  • Whisper speech models are downloaded once from Hugging Face when you first choose Whisper. The request contains no personal data.
  • Claude translation (optional, your own key). If you enter your own Anthropic API key, the subtitle text of the video you are watching is sent to Anthropic's API under your own account so it can be translated. Nothing else is sent. The key is stored in the macOS Keychain and is never transmitted anywhere except to Anthropic. This feature is off unless you turn it on; Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Purchases (the free trial and Aurora Pro) are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no third-party trackers, no ads.
  • No account, no sign-in, no email list.
  • We cannot see which videos you watch, what you translate or what you save.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
